## Seasonal Migration Framework (Franks et al. 2021, *Frontiers in Marine Science*, n=48 sharks)

The landmark western North Atlantic study defines four distinct phases that apply across juveniles, subadults, and adults with largely conserved timing:

| Phase | Period | Location |
|---|---|---|
| **Northward migration** | Mid-May to mid-August (core window May 16–Jun 30) | Corridor: Long Island, NY → Cape Hatteras, NC |
| **Northern summer residency** | **July 1 – October 15** | Cape Cod, Nantucket, Nova Scotia, PEI, New Brunswick, Newfoundland, Grand Banks |
| **Southward migration** | Mid-Sept to early Dec (core Oct 16–Nov 30) | Corridor south |
| **Overwintering residency** | Dec 1 – May 15 | Cape Hatteras → Georgia/Florida → Gulf of Mexico |

**Key timing metrics:**
- Northward migration duration averaged **23.1 days** (SD 16.7); southward **19.7 days** (SD 11.7).
- Arrival to northern grounds varied by year: ~**July 22** (2017–2018) vs. earlier ~**June 26** (2019–2020).
