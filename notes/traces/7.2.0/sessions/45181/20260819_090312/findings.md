## Reading of resolution criteria
- Needs **formal signature by authorized representatives of both governments** of a new
  bilateral Agreement for Cooperation. Announced by State/DOE/Cancillería or credible reporting.
- Fine print: mere **extension** of the 1997 agreement does NOT count.
- Also would NOT count: another MOU/NCMOU (cf. Paraguay), initialing of text, or a joint
  statement announcing "conclusion of negotiations." Only actual signature.
- This makes YES *harder* than a naive read: several plausible near-term news events
  (an MOU, an announcement that negotiations concluded) would still resolve NO.
