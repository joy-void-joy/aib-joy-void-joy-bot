# Q45203: Federal agency model evaluation agreements with BOTH OpenAI and Anthropic, Aug 10 – Sep 3 2026

## Resolution parsing
- Window: **after Aug 10, 2026 and before Sept 3, 2026 ET**. Published Aug 15. Today Aug 19.
  - Explicit start date (Aug 10) precedes published_at (Aug 15) → Step 1b **Case 1**: events Aug 10–15 count if they exist.
- Needs: a U.S. **federal agency** **officially announces** new or **materially expanded** **voluntary agreements** under which a federal agency **evaluates frontier models** from **BOTH OpenAI and Anthropic** for cyber/natsec/capabilities/other explicitly identified frontier-model security risk.
- Fine print: need not be same announcement. Expansions count only if announcement identifies a previously-not-covered model family, access arrangement, testing scope, or evaluation domain.
- Status quo = NO. Requires an affirmative public announcement event within ~24 days (15 remaining).

## Timeline established
- Aug 2024: US AISI announced agreements w/ OpenAI + Anthropic (pre-deployment access). Original baseline.
- ~Aug 2025 (approx): CAISI+UK AISI joint work w/ OpenAI & Anthropic on safeguards; NIST page "CAISI Works with OpenAI and Anthropic to Promote Secure AI Innovation".
- **May 5, 2026**: CAISI announces agreements w/ Google DeepMind, Microsoft, xAI. (OpenAI + Anthropic already had prior arrangements.) — this is the model of what a YES-type announcement looks like.
- **June 2, 2026**: EO 14409 "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security." Voluntary pre-release govt review of "covered frontier models," up to 30-day access window. Sec. 3 framework design due **Aug 1, 2026**; led by **Treasury + NSA (DoW) + CISA (DHS)**, NIST consulting. Explicitly bars mandatory licensing/preclearance.
- Days after EO: WSJ — White House directed **CAISI to STOP issuing public reports** of its model assessments.
- **July 20, 2026**: CAISI director **Chris Fall resigned** after ~3 months. No permanent leadership (Rep. Khanna letter raising alarm).
- July 2026: Anthropic frontier models hit w/ 19-day shutdown via govt export control order; OpenAI GPT-5.6 two-week "gated rollout" under govt limits (ended ~Jul 8).
- **Aug 3–4, 2026**: Framework **finalized behind closed doors**. White House hosted ~a dozen AI companies (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Meta) Tues Aug 4, 30-min meeting to "close the loop."
- **Aug 4, 2026 (Axios)**: **"White House plans to keep AI framework under wraps."** Benchmarks + qualifying thresholds **classified**; framework not publicly released.
- Aug 4 (Axios): Trump AI framework excludes open models.
- Aug 5 (TechPolicy.Press): no company named as having formally signed; ~100 orgs w/ "approved access," criteria unpublished.

## Key structural read
The entire trajectory of this administration's frontier-model review program is toward **secrecy and informality**, not public agreement announcements:
- Framework itself classified/unpublished
- CAISI ordered to stop public assessment reporting
- CAISI leaderless since Jul 20
- Aug 4 meeting was private, 30 min, no public announcement of agreements
- Participation described as informal/pre-existing (OpenAI's Lehane: labs already share info with CAISI informally)

The Aug 4 "close the loop" meeting happened BEFORE the Aug 10 window start — so even the biggest recent event doesn't count.

## YES pathways
1. Post-framework enrollment announcement naming OpenAI + Anthropic (Treasury/NSA/CISA or CAISI) in Aug 10–Sep 3.
2. A CAISI-style press release like May 5 but covering OpenAI + Anthropic with new scope.
3. Separate agency deals (DOE/NNSA, DoW/CDAO) naming both — but must be *evaluation of frontier models for security risk*, not procurement.

## NO pathways
- Framework stays classified; no public enrollment announcements. Companies participate quietly.
- August recess / leadership vacuum → no announcements.
