# Q45182 — Will Israel formally accept the Board of Peace 15-point Gaza plan by Sept 10, 2026?

Published 2026-08-19. Resolves 2026-09-10 23:59 UTC. Horizon: ~22 days.

## Resolution parse
YES requires: announcement, reported by Reuters/credible sources, that Israeli **cabinet, security cabinet, or Netanyahu** has **officially approved, signed, or accepted** the 15-point plan (or its NCAG/withdrawal-linked-to-disarmament framework) as the basis for phase 2.

Explicit NO conditions:
- Israel continues to reject
- Israel accepts only a **materially different** plan
- Negotiations remain frozen
- **Conflicting credible reporting → NO** (tiebreaker favors NO)

Fine print exclusions: general willingness to negotiate, agreeing to attend talks, accepting a ceasefire/pause → **do NOT count**. (This kills the "working groups" pathway.)
Fine print inclusion: a superseding differently-numbered plan counts IF reporting describes Israel as accepting the *same disarmament-linked-to-withdrawal framework*.

Status quo = NO.
