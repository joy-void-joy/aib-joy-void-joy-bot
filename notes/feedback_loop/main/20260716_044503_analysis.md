question-selection-confounded, but it argues against long research packets being the

## Per-Forecast Details

### 44339: West Vancouver Island drought level

- Version: 6.0.0.
- Error type: overconfident MC distribution / missed transition data.
- What happened: the research tool call failed and was abandoned. Direct ArcGIS work
  correctly found the current Level 4, but the forecast put only 4% on Level 2. The
  premortem flagged a fabricated 4→3→4 time series, yet the final answer retained it
  and moved only three points (Level 4 62→65%, Level 3 20→17%).
- What would have helped: query the discovered historical/timelapse ArcGIS services
  and estimate two-week basin transition frequencies before allocating tail mass.

### 44300: NIFC preparedness level

- Version: 6.0.0.
- Error type: directionally wrong / wrong conditional reference class.
- What happened: the research tool call failed and was abandoned. Direct NIFC archive
  research built a 19% unconditional early-July base rate, then excluded the two
  hottest early-escalation years because they had already reached PL4 in June. The
  self-authored counterargument argued 30–35%, but the reviewer independently endorsed
  18–22% and no update occurred.
- What would have helped: retrieve the live IMSR / seven-day significant-fire-potential
  outlook and model the early-PL3, multi-region resource-pressure state rather than
  conditioning only on still being PL3 at July 1.

### 44343: BN wins at least 38 Johor seats

- Version: 6.0.0.
- Error type: wrong base rate / unsupported model parameters.
- What happened: the research tool call failed and was abandoned. The agent created a
  seat Monte Carlo with asserted 0.80 Malay-seat and 0.25 non-Malay-seat win rates;
  the threshold probability was highly sensitive to these unsupported inputs. It did
  not find a 2026 pollster or constituency-level anchor.
- What would have helped: a research pass targeted at local pollsters, party targets,
  and seat-level fundamentals, followed by calibration to 2022 seat outcomes.

### 44303: Russia producer-inclusive diesel export ban

- Version: 6.0.0.
- Error type: timing/catalyst underweight.
- What happened: after the bare-name failure, the agent recovered and received a rich,
  primary-sourced research packet. It knew a dedicated decision meeting was scheduled
  for that day and knew the gasoline precedent could move quickly, but anchored at 14%
  on generic status-quo and ministry-opposition evidence.
- What would have helped: explicitly model P(decision at scheduled meeting) and
  P(signing within window | decision) rather than treating the meeting as one bounded
  positive factor among several correlated status-quo negatives.

### 44352: Moscow Kapotnya refinery

- Version: 6.2.0.
- Error type: wrong resolution-relevant base rate.
- What happened: research returned rich detail. The agent modeled deliberate damaging
  re-strikes on an offline facility, while the criteria also counted Kapotnya-specific
  debris/interception reporting during frequent Moscow raids. Its own counterargument
  described a coherent 25–30% alternative, but the independent reviewer endorsed
  11–15%, and the final stayed at 15%.
- What would have helped: decompose P(Moscow raid in window) × P(Kapotnya named | raid)
  separately from P(deliberate damaging strike).

## Model and Architecture Comparison

- v5.0.0 has no git tag; commit `2c0fc2b4` sets the version. v6.0.0 is tagged.
- Between the v5.0.0 version commit and v6.0.0, `prompts.py`, `tool_policy.py`, and
  research/subforecast tool implementations are unchanged. The material forecasting
  change is `claude-opus-4-6` → `claude-opus-4-8`; an unrelated reviewer wording edit
  and tournament configuration change also occurred.
- Opus 4.6's 06/15 cohort had zero bare-name `research` failures and 52/54 scored
  forecasts used successful research. Opus 4.8's 06/29 cohort had 22 bare-name
  failures. Most recovered, but five abandoned research, and those five contain three
  of the four catastrophic misses.
- v6.1.0 fixed the prompt/tool documentation to expose the exact callable name. Thus
  the evidence points to a model × tool-contract interaction, not a clean intrinsic
  4.8 forecasting-quality effect.

## Trace Quality

- The premortem is generative but weakly corrective. Across 36 binary 06/29 forecasts,
  mean absolute pre/post change was only 0.33 percentage points; no forecast moved by
  more than five points. Warn verdicts averaged a 0.54-point change.
- In the 06/15 cohort, the corresponding mean was 0.52 points overall and 2.21 points
  for warns: still small, but more corrective than 06/29.
- The reviewer often validates the anchor even when the same agent has authored a
  materially different counterargument. The gate verifies that dissent was generated;
  it does not force an adjudicated posterior.
- There were zero actual `subforecast()` calls in either MiniBench cohort.
- The worldview store currently has 133 flat research entries and 153 forecast entries,
  all at depth 0. The schema provides timestamps, tags, parent pointers, and outcomes,
  but no explicit causal/dependency edges, conditional distributions, evidence weights,
  or propagation mechanism. It is a cache plus maintenance sweep, not yet a Bayesian
  network.

## Tracking Gaps

- Forecast JSON has no tournament/cohort field. The 06/29 cohort was reconstructed from
  the API slug and contiguous post IDs; it cannot be selected reliably from local
  forecast files alone.
- Forecast JSON records `model: null`; Opus version is inferred from agent version and
  historical config rather than recorded at runtime.
- v5.0.0 lacks an immutable git tag.
- Research packets do not record which facts the orchestrator actually consumed in its
  factors, so “grounding” cannot be measured directly.

## Open Questions and Proposed Experiments

1. Run a same-question, cutoff-safe factorial retrodiction on a stratified 06/29 sample:
   Opus 4.6 vs 4.8, exact callable names fixed, same commit and tool surface. This
   separates model quality from the tool-name interaction.
2. Add an ablation for research return shape: full prose packet vs compact evidence
   cards with full detail persisted out-of-context. Measure Brier/log score, factual
   support coverage, and evidence actually cited in final factors.
3. Replace self-authored counterargument + validating reviewer with a blind independent
   alternative forecast and explicit adjudication. Instrument both estimates and the
   posterior movement.
4. Test a decomposition compiler that creates a small local dependency graph before
   research; do not rely on a prompt directive. Track subforecast invocation, reuse,
   resolution, and contribution to the parent estimate.
5. Add `scores cohort <slug>`, stamp tournament slug and runtime model into every saved
   forecast, fix binary/categorical worldview resolution persistence, and tag v5.0.0's
   historical commit.
